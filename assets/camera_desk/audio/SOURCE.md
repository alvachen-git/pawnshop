The author identifies the recording as a 1935 Contax rangefinder fired at several speeds. It is not a Leica I recording. The publicly served MP3 preview is retained unchanged under source/; it is not the login-only original WAV.

Edits: extract two complete exposure sounds (15.125–15.365 and 21.815–22.320 seconds), mix stereo to mono, use a common 0.80 gain, apply short edge fades, encode 48 kHz PCM16 WAV. No synthetic tones or replacement Foley. tools/build_camera_audio.py reproduces the edits.

Normal clips preserve the recorded sequence. The delayed and jammed variants are game sound edits of this recording: insert a pause after a quiet recorded button sound, or stop the recording before the full action completes. They do not claim to document an actual broken Contax.
